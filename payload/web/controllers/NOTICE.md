# Controller reference artwork

These four WebP images and `layouts.json` were extracted from the owner-supplied interactive controller reference HTML. The owner confirmed permission to publish them in this repository on 2026-10-10. No separate open asset license or original photographer attribution was supplied. Do not infer GPL or CC0 licensing for these images from the code license. Steam, Valve, PlayStation and DualSense names/logos remain their respective owners’ trademarks.

The images are unchanged. Logical `iw`/`ih` and `vb` coordinates in `layouts.json` are deliberately preserved even when decoded WebP dimensions differ. Back-view physical L is visually on the right and R on the left. Source details and exact SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `docs/PROVENANCE.md`.
